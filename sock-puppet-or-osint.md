### Privacy and Email Management

* Duck.com / DuckDuckGo Email Plugin: Used to generate unlimited unique email aliases to prevent data brokers from tracking a primary address.
* Proton (Proton Mail): Recommended for true privacy as it allows for dedicated email accounts not tied to other services.
* Simple Login: Mentioned as a proxy email service that may have vulnerabilities but is useful for managing junk signups.
* Apple’s "Hide My Email": A service mentioned in the context of recent privacy patches.
