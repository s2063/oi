#### Using Banking Data for Phone Number Owner Verification

Traditional phone number investigations often rely on apps like Truecaller, which return unreliable, user-generated nicknames and stale data. A more accurate method leverages financial platforms—like digital wallets and global remittance apps—because strict Know Your Customer (KYC) compliance laws require them to link phone numbers to legally verified identities. By initiating a mock transfer to a target's number on these banking apps, investigators can securely uncover the person's true, legally verified name instead of a crowdsourced alias.
