# Books Search | OSINT

A curated list of free book and document search engines for OSINT research — including Anna's Archive, Library Genesis, Z-Library, and more.

_Find and download books, research papers, and academic texts using these open-source intelligence resources. These tools are widely used by researchers, students, and OSINT practitioners to locate hard-to-find publications._
